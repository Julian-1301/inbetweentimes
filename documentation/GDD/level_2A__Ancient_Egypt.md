# Synopsis

After [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n)'s arrival in Egypt he notices a suspicious man leaving [The Egyptian Tomb](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/111cdc3e-d84a-420e-9bf3-d9804c43bf3a?n). He doesn't hesitate for a second and walks towards the tomb located next to the oasis. After inspecting the entrance he notices the entrance is blocked off and he is required to solve a series of puzzles to solve the mystery of the missing scarab.

# Objectives

Solve the following puzzles and solve the mystery.

<br>

### Oasis Water Puzzle:

"The oasis holds a series of magical pools, each representing a different element. You must determine the correct order to interact with these pools based on clues you get by interacting with objects around the level. After doing this a hidden panel opens up and reveals an item that looks way too advanced to be used in this era as well as a large hyroglyphic inscription that you can't seem to translate"

<br>

### Hieroglyphic Code Breaker:

"After collecting the hyrogliphic decoder tablet you can use it to translate the inscription on the wall. It tells you to do the following:

- Light the left torch, keep the other two unlit
- Plant a seed in the driest place and give it some water
- Press the cracked tile three times

Only after doing all of this will you hear a voice coming from one of the Anubis statues"

<br>

### Anubis Riddles:

The two Anubis statues will take turns asking a sequence of riddles, they will not tell you how many you got right and wrong, they will only open the door for you once you succesfully got every riddle right. These questions can be solved purely by general knowledge but people who struggled with history can find answers around the different rooms.

<br>

# Level walkthrough

Start by looking around the level, clues for the oasis puzzle can be found by inspecting everything in the area and will lead you to push the buttons in the following order: Earth, Air, Fire, Water. After doing this you will receive a button that helps you out in [Level 2B: Cold War Submarine](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/7c40cefd-0899-49a7-bb86-781db4640f1e?n), go here and solve the puzzles there until you receive the decoder tablet. Return back to [The Egyptian Tomb](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/111cdc3e-d84a-420e-9bf3-d9804c43bf3a?n) and use the tablet on the hieroglyphs. You can find a lighter in [The Cold War Submarine](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/5aa7615d-001e-4d31-a1ba-124fbc86acb2?n), use this on the left torch and keep the others unlit. You can find the seed in your office by interacting with the potted plant. You can find a cup in a pile of sand on the left side of [The Egyptian Tomb](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/111cdc3e-d84a-420e-9bf3-d9804c43bf3a?n). Fill the cup with water from the oasis and use both the seed and the cup on the dry area on the right side of the level. After doing this solve the riddles from the Anubis statues. Inspect the opened tomb door and return to your office.

# General description

In the ancient Egyptian desert, a sacred tomb guarded by formidable jackal statues stands as a silent sentinel. Adorned with hieroglyphs, the tomb whispers tales of pharaohs and the afterlife. Nearby, a small oasis mirrors the life-giving waters of the Nile, adding an oasis of serenity to this mystical and ancient Egyptian location.

Within the confines of the ancient Egyptian tomb, concealed amid the shadows and hieroglyphic tales, lies the golden scarab. This elusive artifact, adorned with cryptic symbols, is said to hold the power to unleash chaos upon the world when combined with two other ancient artefacts. [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) is tasked with retrieving this ancient artefact before it gets into the wrong hands.

# Backstory

The followers of the doomwhisperers' cult refuse to live according to the societal norm in the new world and have been inspired by [Lilith](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/bd1a11a1-9f08-41c9-817b-9e6ce34da218?n) to force mayhem upon this utopian civilization. These anarchists refuse to listen to any government, yet sheepishly follow every single command their precious leader [Lilith](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/bd1a11a1-9f08-41c9-817b-9e6ce34da218?n) gives them. This has resulted in them overtaking important organisations spread throughout the timeline and they will unbeknowingly help lilith spread way more chaos than they could ever imagine by summoning a chaos god.

# Personality

[Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) will only meet one other member of the cult besides [Lilith](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/bd1a11a1-9f08-41c9-817b-9e6ce34da218?n) throughout the story and he can be described the same way as all the other members: mysterious, devoted, strong-minded and most of all extremely anti-everything.

# Appearance

The cultists wear a green robe and cover their face with masks to keep their identity hidden. They like to strike fear into people by making their entire outfit look old and dated, almost mummy-like to make people believe they are dealing with ancient terrors.

# Working title

Inbetween Times

# Elevator pitch

We’re developing a **text-based RPG adventure game** set in a modern fantasy world inspired by worldwide myths and folklore. The gameplay is strongly story-driven and draws inspiration from the Netflix series “Bodies.” Imagine a fusion of time travel and the investigative prowess of “Sherlock Holmes.”

## Theme and setting

**Inbetween Times** is a text-based game set **in the 25th century**. As a detective, you face a monumental task—one that has already occurred in the past. Your mission involves **time travel**, taking you to various historical periods to unravel a mystery. The locations you’ll explore include ancient Egypt, the tense era of the Cold War, and the vibrant Aztec civilization. The protagonist is a man in his thirties.

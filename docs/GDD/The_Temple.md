# General description

Hidden deep within the caverns of the Caucasus Mountains lies an eerie and secluded temple, shrouded in the mists of ancient times. Millennia ago, it served as a sacrificial holy site, where people offered homage to long-forgotten gods. Few stumbled upon its hidden entrance, either by chance or fate, for the temple chose its visitors. Of those who dared to enter, only a handful returned, forever altered by the experience. They emerged as mere shadows of their former selves, consumed by hatred and madness.

Within the temple's crumbling walls now dwell the enigmatic cult known as the Doomwhisperers, practitioners of dark and forbidden rituals. They harness the ancient power that permeates the temple's stone, whispering doom and despair into the hearts of those who dare to trespass upon their domain.

# Backstory

Elias entered the world three decades ago, welcomed into a life of modest comfort and familial bliss. His childhood was idyllic, filled with laughter, love, and the simple pleasures of rural living. Yet, at the tender age of sixteen, the tranquility shattered. A malevolent force, driven by a vendetta shrouded in mystery, descended upon his serene village, leaving a wake of devastation in its path. Elias's world crumbled as he witnessed the untimely demise of his beloved parents, victims of a cruel twist of fate orchestrated by an unknown assailant.

In the aftermath of tragedy, Elias found solace in a newfound purpose: to safeguard the fabric of time itself, ensuring that others would not suffer the same heartache he endured. With steely determination, he dedicated his life to the pursuit of justice and the prevention of temporal anomalies. Upon reaching the age of twenty, Elias sought out the enigmatic organization known only as "the Agency" — clandestine guardians tasked with policing the tenuous boundaries of time travel.

Under the tutelage of seasoned mentors, Elias honed his intellect and sharpened his instincts, emerging as one of the agency's most skilled operatives. As a seasoned detective, he traversed the labyrinth of time, unraveling the intricacies of temporal disturbances and thwarting nefarious plots that threatened to unravel the very fabric of existence.

A decade has passed since Elias first donned the mantle of a temporal guardian. Yet, as he receives a summons from his superiors, a sense of foreboding grips him. This latest anomaly beckons him into uncharted territory, hinting at a conspiracy that may test his resolve like never before. Will Elias unearth the truth behind this enigmatic disturbance, or will he succumb to the shadows lurking just beyond the veil of time? Only time will tell...

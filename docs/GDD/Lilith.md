# Backstory

In the shadows of the world, where chaos and secrecy intertwine, there emerged a female cult leader known as Lilith Nightshade, the enigmatic force behind the clandestine cult of chaos called Doomwhispers. Lilith's journey into darkness began in her early years when she experienced the reality of a perfect society but unlike the majority of people it bored her. She wanted it to be more chaoitc, more pure.&#x20;

Driven by a need to make the world more chaotic, Lilith sought solace in the forbidden knowledge hidden within ancient tomes and esoteric texts. As she delved deeper into the realms of forbidden wisdom, she discovered the chaotic forces that permeate the fabric of reality itself. This revelation ignited a spark within her, and she became a conduit for the very chaos she sought to understand.

Lilith's charisma and persuasive rhetoric drew like-minded individuals to her cause, and she formed Doomwhisperers—a cult that embraced chaos as a means of transcending societal constraints. The cult attracted those disillusioned by the structured order of the world, offering them an alternative path that promised liberation through anarchy.

Lilith, with her piercing gaze and magnetic presence, became a beacon of inspiration for her followers. She wove a tapestry of chaos, convincing her disciples that only by embracing the unpredictable nature of existence could they truly break free from the chains that bound them. The cult engaged in clandestine rituals, tapping into forbidden energies to amplify the chaos they sought to unleash upon the world.

Under Lilith's guidance, Doomwhispers infiltrated all kinds of organisations all around history this was for only one purpose to undermine the peace the people had in her current time, but she also had a hidden purpose to summon the god of chaos&#x20;

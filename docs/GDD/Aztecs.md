# General description

In this era, you find yourself within an Aztec temple hidden deep in a jungle in the heart of South America. The temple is sacred to the local population and has claimed numerous lives, primarily those of Spanish conquerors who were sacrificed to the gods. At times, even the lives of people residing around the temple, especially criminals, were offered.

Your mission is to locate a valuable Aztec artifact - a small statue dedicated to one of their gods. This statue holds immeasurable value for the Aztecs and is guarded by a series of intricate puzzles related to both the Spanish invasion and Aztec rituals and traditions.

As you venture deeper into the temple, you will face challenges. If you successfully solve all these puzzles, the sacred door of the temple will open, allowing you to secure the coveted Aztec statue.

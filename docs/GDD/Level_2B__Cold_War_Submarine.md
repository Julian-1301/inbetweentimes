# Synopsis

As the [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) steps into the submarine, he's immediately awestruck by the intricate complexity of the metal behemoth surrounding him. It's a tubular control room, bathed in the dull glow of machinery, its walls adorned with a labyrinth of pipes and panels. Peering through a nearby window, he's met with the surreal sight of the deep ocean depths outside—realizing in an instant that he's submerged underwater.

Examining the insignias adorning the interior, he discerns unmistakable Soviet markings, indicating the vessel's origin. The revelation only deepens the mystery: why has this anomaly occurred within the confines of a Soviet submarine?

As he delves deeper into the vessel's secrets, the Detective is driven by a relentless pursuit of truth, determined to uncover the answers behind this enigmatic occurrence.

# Objectives

The objective is to uncover why the time traveler(s) went to this submarine. What where they after, what is missing?

1. **The Ciphered Logbook:**
   - [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) notices an logbook containing encryptated texts. Within the book there is a loose page containing alot of dates with assigned decryption keys
   - [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) deduces that he needs to find out which date it is to find out what the logbook says.
   - When [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) solves the puzzle the logbook states that there is a fire in one of the missle tubes and the ships hydraulics have been sobataged to steer the submarine to the coast of the USA.
     <br>
2. **The Hydraulic Puzzle:**
   - [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) discovers that the hydraulic control panel is malfunctioning, affecting the submarine's navigation systems.
   - As he investigates further, he realizes that the valves are twisted randomly and are stuck in place. the control panel seems to be missing the release button, making it impossible to move the valves.
   - [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) searches the control room but cannot find the missing button. However, he notices clues near the panel that seem to be related to the missing button.
   - He deduces that the missing button must be located somewhere else.
   - [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) is able to retrieve the button in [level 2A: Ancient Egypt](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/33258b6b-4c45-4aba-92d8-ad06ce07439e?n).
   - After the button has been found and placed and [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) restored functionality to the hydraulics a strange object falls from behind the hydraulics, it looks like an ancient tablet ([level 2A: Ancient Egypt](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/33258b6b-4c45-4aba-92d8-ad06ce07439e?n)).
     <br>
   **Star Map Puzzle:**
   - [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) notices a series of drawings pinned to a wall in the control room. Upon closer inspection, he realizes that these drawings depict constellations at different points in time.
   - The dates are written beneath each drawing, suggesting they represent the night sky on those specific dates.
   - After studying the drawings, [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) notices a pattern: the positions of certain stars shift gradually over time due to the Earth's rotation. By comparing the positions of key stars in the different drawings, he can deduce the approximate date.
   - Once he has determined the date using the star map puzzle, [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) can then proceed to decipher the logbook.

After the puzzles are solved [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) will realize that this submarine is the Soviet K-219 nuclear submarine containing nuclear missles. He states to himself that he should check the main control panel. Here he sees that one of the nuclear launch keys is missing...

# Level walkthrough

1. **Ciphered Logbook:**
   - Start by examining the logbook containing encrypted texts. Note the loose page with dates and decryption keys.
   - Decrypt the text corresponding to the current date using the decryption keys provided.
   - Discover the message about the fire in a missile tube and the sabotage of the submarine's hydraulics.
2. **Hydraulic Puzzle:**
   - Investigate the malfunctioning hydraulic control panel in the submarine's control room.
   - Notice the twisted valves and the missing release button.
   - Search the control room for clues related to the missing button.
   - Realize that the missing button is located elsewhere, specifically in level 2A: Ancient Egypt.
   - Retrieve the missing button from level 2A and return to the control room.
   - Install the missing button and restore functionality to the hydraulics.
   - Find an ancient tablet that falls from behind the hydraulics, also from level 2A: Ancient Egypt.
     <br>
3. **Star Map Puzzle:**
   - Examine the series of drawings pinned to a wall in the control room depicting constellations at different points in time.
   - Study the dates written beneath each drawing to determine the approximate date represented by each constellation.
   - Notice the shifting positions of certain stars over time due to the Earth's rotation.
   - Compare the positions of key stars in the different drawings to deduce the approximate current date.
     <br>
4. **Conclusion:**
   - Realize that the submarine is the Soviet K-219 nuclear submarine containing nuclear missiles.
   - Check the main control panel and observe that one of the nuclear launch keys is missing.

# General description

Amidst the tension of the Cold War, the specter of nuclear annihilation loomed larger than ever before. Humanity teetered on the brink of catastrophe, with multiple incidents bringing the world perilously close to the brink. Had history unfolded differently, our existence would have plunged into an era of chaos and turmoil.

It is within this tumultuous backdrop that the cult known as the 'Doomwhispers' seeks a relic of this precarious time—a nuclear launch key.

This location lies within the confines of the Soviet K-219 nuclear submarine, a vessel capable of unleashing devastating intercontinental ballistic missiles. Sunk by an explosion approximately 2200 kilometers off the coast of the USA, the submarine's demise was, fortunately, the result of a conventional blast. Nevertheless, it claimed the lives of numerous crew members and stood as a stark reminder of the catastrophic potential that loomed over the world during that era.

# General description

In the middle of a bustling 25th century city lies a detective's office that looks like it has been stuck in time for centuries. [Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) has always been more impressed with the past than the future and decided his base of operation should reflect this.&#x20;

[Detective Elias Hawthorne](https://app.nuclino.com/t/b/0a1b60df-d4f4-49b5-86fa-d54f41793882?n) goes back to his office after collecting items and clues in different levels to investigate these further in his office.

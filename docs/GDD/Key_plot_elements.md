1. **Establish Motivation**: As a seasoned time-traveler detective employed by a secret organization, "the Agency", the protagonist receives a call from his boss alerting them to anomalies disrupting the timeline.
2. **Clarity and Detail**: During his investigations, the protagonist uncovers cryptic messages left behind by a mysterious group, hinting at their sinister motives and the importance of the artifacts they seek.
3. **Progression and Pacing**: After successfully resolving the first two anomalies, the protagonist is thrust into a third mission without respite, highlighting the urgency of their task.
4. **Foreshadowing**: Throughout his journey, the protagonist encounters subtle clues hinting at the existence of a fanatical cult known as the "Doom Whisperers," who worship ancient entities and seek to bring back chaos and turmoil.
5. **Visual Imagery**: As the protagonist arrives at the temple, he is greeted by an ominous scene: swirling mists, flickering torches, and hooded figures chanting in an ancient tongue, setting the stage for a climactic showdown.
6. **Consequences and Impact**: As the ritual reaches its crescendo, with a chilling smile the cult leader reveals that the protagonist's unwitting actions have paved the way for the summoning of a malevolent entity. The protagonist is struck on the back of the head, plunging the world into darkness as the creature emerges from the depths.

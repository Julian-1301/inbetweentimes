On the 26th of March 2083, a date etched in the annals of history as the end and the beginning, humanity faced its darkest hour. A cataclysmic event of unprecedented scale swept across the globe, claiming millions of lives and leaving civilization teetering on the brink of oblivion. Some speak of a global war waged with devastating weapons, while others whisper of a cosmic catastrophe or a technological meltdown. Whatever its nature, this calamity marked humanity's nadir, a moment of reckoning that would forever alter the course of our species.

In the aftermath of this harrowing trial, humanity found itself at a crossroads. People from all walks of life united in a Herculean effort to rebuild our shattered world. The journey was fraught with challenges, as rival factions vied for power, resources dwindled, and ideologies clashed. Yet, through perseverance and resilience, we emerged from the ashes, forging a new era of progress and innovation.

By the year 2419, our civilization has reached unparalleled heights of technological prowess. Interstellar travel, once the realm of science fiction, is now a reality, opening up boundless vistas of exploration and discovery. The tantalizing prospect of time travel, too, has become a tangible possibility, although its temptations are tempered by the collective wisdom of our society. Recognizing the dangers inherent in tampering with the fabric of time, humanity enacted a strict ban on such endeavors. Yet, despite these prohibitions, there are those who dare to defy the laws of causality, driven by motives both noble and nefarious.

Enter the Agency—a clandestine organization tasked with safeguarding the integrity of the temporal continuum. Their mission: to track down rogue time travelers and rectify the disruptions they leave in their wake. Employing advanced technologies and covert operatives, the Agency operates in the shadows, vigilant against the machinations of those who would seek to alter history for their own ends.

But even as we stand on the cusp of a glorious future, shadows stir in the depths of the human psyche. A murmuring of dissent echoes through the corridors of power, as whispers spread of a clandestine cabal lurking in the shadows—a cult dedicated to chaos and upheaval. Rumors abound of their clandestine gatherings, their arcane rituals, and their insidious agenda to plunge humanity once more into the abyss. Are they merely the fevered imaginings of a paranoid populace, or do darker forces indeed lurk beneath the veneer of civilization?
